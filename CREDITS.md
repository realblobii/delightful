## Display Delight (LGPL-3.0)

The placeable food blocks (blocks `placed_*`, models and textures under `block/dd/`, the Roast definitions in `recipe/displaydelight/`) are
carried from **Display Delight** by jkvin114, https://github.com/jkvin114/display-delight-neoforge, **licensed LGPL-3.0** (mod code, five model
modifications and its other models) with five models derived from **Farmers 3D by Pder** (MIT). The full text is in `licenses/LICENSE-displaydelight.txt`.
Those files stay under the LGPL: you may modify and redistribute them under its terms, and they are plain data files in the `.hmod` (a zip) so they
can be replaced. The rest of Delightful is MIT. Nothing of Display Delight's Java code was used.

**To confirm:** the repositories carry an MIT licence for the code. None stated a separate licence for artwork, but a mod's
art may still belong to its artists: if an author objects, the content is removed. Expanded Delight ships no
`LICENSE` file at the root of its repository (its `gradle.properties` says MIT); see `licenses/NOTICE-expandeddelight.txt`.
